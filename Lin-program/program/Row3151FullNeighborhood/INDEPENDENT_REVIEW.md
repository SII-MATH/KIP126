No correctness findings in the reviewed four Lean source files and their
imported six finite families.

The incoming-source d3 map is `[0,a]` with zero incoming differential.
Its kernel has dimension 2 for `a=false` and 1 for `a=true`. The imported
projection sends its named first-prefix vector to the first E4 basis vector
in both cases. The new event comparison uses exactly that dependent
dimension. In particular, it does not infer `a=true` from a premise stating
that the previously known prefix already spans the whole kernel.

The known event and eta coordinate constraint make the event outgoing
matrix `[b,1;0,0]`. Its kernel is the line generated by `(1,b)`. Vanishing
on the first incoming-prefix vector makes the first incoming column zero.
For dimension 1 this forces the whole incoming map to be zero. For dimension
2 the other column may be either zero or `(1,b)`, giving the four `a=false`
cases. Together with the two `a=true` cases there are exactly six.

The nonzero incoming cases `001` and `011` are retained. Their incoming
image is the entire outgoing kernel, so the event's next homology group is
zero-dimensional. The named event source is `(0,1)` and is not in that
kernel; its image remains `(1,0)`. Thus zero next-page homology does not
invalidate the claimed nonzero current-page differential.

## Scope of exhaustiveness

`Semantics.all_admissible_matrices` proves exhaustiveness of the event
outgoing/incoming pair under the explicit dimension, known event, eta,
prefix, and complex conditions. `Checks.complete_window` separately proves
that each selected case has the specified coherent nine-key family with
coverage of all three named degrees on pages 2, 3, and 4. These results do
not say that every matrix at every one of the nine keys can vary arbitrarily
and is included in six choices, nor that arbitrary nine-key completions are
unique.

The legacy `Row3151BranchCertificates` four-case result accepts
`inc : Matrix 2 1`, fixes its column to zero, and varies both unknown
outgoing bits. Its claimed exhaustiveness is limited to that incoming
dimension. The new result imposes eta's second-coordinate constraint and
also covers incoming dimension 2; it correctly supplies the previously
missing nonzero incoming possibilities.

The target d4 comparison equals the previously checked
`Row3152BranchCertificates` source comparison for the same `b`. Raw E2
endpoint paths and the common differential are bound to the same family.
The theorem is still a finite algebraic result: no typed identification of
an arbitrary actual Adams nine-key window is supplied. Source provenance
and retained NULL rows do not replace those interpretation obligations.
