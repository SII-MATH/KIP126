\chapter{External computed results}
\label{chap:computed-inputs}

This chapter maintains the individual model-bound computation inputs of
Challenge2. Each numbered statement is linked to its exact delivery field and
its fixed LinProgram data or mathematical comparison. The chapter has 33
numbered statements and uses the same flat format as the literature inventory.
The shared notation is unnumbered; internal applications, examples, appendix
transcriptions and source audits are recorded in their own chapters.

\section{Individual Challenge2 computation statements}
\label{sec:computation-master-inventory}

The computation delivery has \textbf{29 individual result statements}, after
expanding its nested proposition records, and \textbf{4 comparison statements}
in its bindings. The flat index below lists these 33 statements once each.
Parameterized families count as one statement, and conjunctions within a
field retain all their clauses. Coordinate choices, interpretations and
assembly records are data, rather than additional numbered inputs. The seven
top-level result fields are packaging for these 29 statements, not seven
individual theorem entries. Four label equations are definitional comparisons;
this is a count of explicit statement fields, not logically independent facts.

Each input has its own mathematical node and direct Lean projection link.
The canonical \texttt{docs/external-inputs.json} manifest records the owning
field, sources and locators. All input nodes retain their unfinished delivery
status. Document correspondence, a successful import, a checksum and a
successful compilation do not certify the claims on the fixed Adams towers.
Raw-data definitions and the shared binding definition are in
Chapter~\ref{chap:computation-schema}. Appendix transcription and export
coverage are audited in Chapter~\ref{chap:coverage-audit}; internal
consequences are in Chapter~\ref{chap:near126}.

Write $A(s,t)=A^{s,t}$ and $\mathcal R_H$ for Def's specified mod--2 ring
structure on $H$. In cycle notation, $C_{T_{\mathrm{fix}}}^{s,t}$ abbreviates
$C_{T_{\mathrm{fix}}.X}^{s,t}$.

The route's object identifiers evaluate to precisely $S^0$ and
$C\nu=\operatorname{cofib}(\nu_{\mathrm{Hopf}}:S^3\to S^0)$,
where $\nu_{\mathrm{Hopf}}=D.\mathrm{auxiliary}.\mathrm{nuMap}$.
Its basis data are $R_{o,s,t,i}\in E_2^{s,t}(o)$.
The partial coordinate decoder $\operatorname{dec}_R(o,s,t,I)$ first
requires a matching entry of the fixed degree list, strictly increasing
indices, and every index below that entry's monomial-list length. On
success its value is $\sum_{i\in I}R_{o,s,t,i}$; otherwise it is undefined.
In particular, an empty coordinate list at a missing degree does not decode.

Write $\operatorname{CSV}_{s,t}(x,I)$ if there is a list of actual rows
of the fixed sphere CSV basis table whose local-index list is exactly $I$,
every row has degree $(s,t)$, and $|x|$ is the sum of those rows' literal
monomial values. This uses the table's prescribed row membership and
degree, rather than an independently chosen coordinate map.
For either actual classical sequence, write $\operatorname{rep}_r(x,u)$
if $r\ge2$ and one raw cycle at index $r-2$ has initial $E_2$ quotient
$x$ and $r$-page quotient $u$. Define the differential relation
\[
  \mathcal D_r^o(p,q;x,y)\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \begin{gathered}
    q=p+\operatorname{diffDeg}(r),\quad
    \exists u\in E_r^p(o),\ v\in E_r^q(o),\\
    \operatorname{rep}_r(x,u)\ \land\operatorname{rep}_r(y,v)
       \ \land\ d_ru=v,
  \end{gathered}
\]
where the last equality uses that actual degree transport.
Also write $\operatorname{Reach}_r^o(p,x)$ for
$\exists u\in E_r^p(o),\operatorname{rep}_r(x,u)$, and
$\operatorname{Bound}_r^o(p,x)$ for $r\ge2$ and existence of an element
of the actual raw boundary subobject at index $r-1$ whose image under
the fixed inclusions and initial $E_2$ quotient is $x$.
These definitions allow zero page classes and zero differential targets.


\begin{theorem}[The delivered sphere coordinate vectors have their prescribed CSV values]
  \label{thm:computation-sphereBasis-csv-values}
  \lean{KIP126.Challenge2.SphereBasisInterface.csv_values,KIP126.Challenge2.ComputationResults.sphereBasis}
  \notready
  \uses{def:computation-fixed-bindings}
  Let $\chi_{s,t}:E_2^{s,t}(S^0)\cong
  (\mathrm{BasisIndex}(s,t)\to_0\mathbb F_2)$ be the integer-linear
  coordinate equivalence supplied by the same result delivery's
  \texttt{sphereBasis.coordinates}. For all $s,t\in\mathbb N$ with
  $t\le261$ and every $i\in\mathrm{BasisIndex}(s,t)$,
  \[
    \left|P_{s,t}^{-1}\bigl(\chi_{s,t}^{-1}(\delta_i)\bigr)\right|
        =\mathrm{basisValue}(\mathrm{basisRowAt}(s,t,i)),
  \]
  where $\delta_i$ is the finite-support coordinate vector with value
  one at $i$ and zero elsewhere. The coordinate data and this consistency
  condition belong to one delivery; the condition is one quantified
  statement for the full bound, rather than a theorem for each CSV row.
\end{theorem}

\begin{theorem}[Every located sphere staircase row decodes and has its stated mathematical meaning]
  \label{thm:computation-sphereStaircase-rows-sound}
  \lean{KIP126.Challenge2.SphereStaircaseInterface.rows_sound,KIP126.Challenge2.ComputationResults.sphereStaircase}
  \lean{KIP126.Challenge2.StaircaseClaimStatement,KIP126.Computation.LinProofs.State.decode}
  \notready
  \uses{def:computation-fixed-bindings}
  For all shard and offset indices in $\mathbb N$ and every raw staircase
  row $w$, if the fixed staircase lookup at those indices returns $w$,
  there exists a claim $c$ with $\mathrm{decode}(w)=\mathrm{some}(c)$,
  and $c$ satisfies the corresponding one of the following conditions
  through the same $P$:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item For $c=\mathrm{equation}(r,s,t,I,J)$, there exist witnesses
      $t\le261$, $t+r-1\le261$, $x\in A(s,t)$ and
      $y\in A(s+r,t+r-1)$ such that
      $\operatorname{CSV}_{s,t}(x,I)$,
      $\operatorname{CSV}_{s+r,t+r-1}(y,J)$, and
      $\mathcal D_r^{S^0}((s,t),(s+r,t+r-1);P_{s,t}x,P_{s+r,t+r-1}y)$.
    \item For $c=\mathrm{reaches}(r,s,t,I)$, there exist $t\le261$
      and $x\in A(s,t)$ with $\operatorname{CSV}_{s,t}(x,I)$ and
      $\operatorname{Reach}_r^{S^0}((s,t),P_{s,t}x)$.
    \item For $c=\mathrm{boundaryBy}(r,s,t,I)$, there exist $t\le261$
      and $x\in A(s,t)$ with $\operatorname{CSV}_{s,t}(x,I)$ and
      $\operatorname{Bound}_r^{S^0}((s,t),P_{s,t}x)$.
  \end{enumerate}
  The claim indices are natural numbers. Both decoding success and the
  appropriate mathematical conclusion are required. An unresolved outgoing
  row only asserts a common page representative, and an unresolved incoming
  row only asserts cumulative boundary membership. The decoder's threshold
  claim $\mathrm{reaches}(1000,s,t,I)$ has exactly that finite meaning.
  The whole table is one quantified statement.
\end{theorem}
