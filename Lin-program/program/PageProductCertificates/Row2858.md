# Row2858 in actual finite quotient spaces

prepare_row2858.py exports six actual bilinear product tensors: multiplication
by g,h1,h3 both on the initial degree(10,136) and on its d3 target(13,138).
It loads full neighboring d2 complexes for all three one-dimensional factors,
all product sources and targets. The polynomial coefficient checker accepts
all six witness bundles and Row2858.lean proves their validity via lin_cert.
No fixed-factor ring valuation or boundary faithfulness is assumed.

The resulting actual Quot maps kill the named initial class h0^2*x126,8,3;
the zero product identities are proved at the quotient level from the actual
tensors. On the three-dimensional target quotient, the combined three maps
reflect zero (combined_zero_reflects). Its proof invokes the earlier full
boundary-span comparisons and finite candidate elimination. Since these are
bilinear quotient maps, zero reflection is the kernel criterion for their
combined injectivity; the current theorem exposes precisely zero reflection.

Finally differential_named_zero takes local differential maps at these fixed
spaces, their zero preservation, and three commuting Leibniz squares for all
initial classes. It deduces d(named)=0. The squares have the factor-cycle terms
already removed: they must be supplied from actual Leibniz plus d(g)=d(h1)=
d(h3)=0; h1 has the separate quotient-level H1QuotientLeibniz theorem. This
conditional differential theorem does not assign zero to a NULL, claim the
factor d3 facts unconditionally, or prove an Adams comparison.

The module compiles directly with Lean -j1. row2858_review.py independently
checks three factor input triples and six tensor matrices against stored
source records and existing actual product provenance, recording DB SHA256.
